Frequently asked questions

What people ask before signing

What does Inspector Hacker include?

Continuous monitoring: an inventory of everything exposed to the internet, AI review of your code and of your server and cloud configuration, a threat feed for your technology, and a monthly report with the list of what a person should review, explained on a call.

Is it a pentest?

No. A manual pentest of a single application costs $3,000 to $18,000 USD and is done by certified people; we quote it separately with a partner, per day. What we sell is monitoring: the agents run every day and a human does the reading. We never launch attacks against production.

How much does it cost?

Onboarding $2,000 USD one time. Vigía $900 USD a month (1 server, 1 repository and website). Vigía Plus $1,800 USD a month (up to 5 servers, several apps, cloud and identity). Enterprise from $3,500 USD a month. Same price in the United States and Mexico.

What access do you need?

Read-only: a read-only user on GitHub or GitLab, a read-only SSH key, read access to the cloud and to DNS. Everything is written in the contract and you authorize every scan. No write access and no changes without your approval.

Do you serve the United States?

Yes. We operate in the United States (Bentonville, Arkansas and San Antonio, Texas as our base) and in Mexico (Torreón and the whole country), with the monthly call in English or Spanish.

Why AI monitoring and not just an antivirus?

Because AI-driven attacks already find flaws and write exploits in minutes. If the attack takes seconds, the monitoring has to be AI too: weekly scans of what is exposed, alerts within 24 hours on a critical vulnerability of your technology, and a review of every change that lands in your code.
