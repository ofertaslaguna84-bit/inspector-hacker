Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers

Why are prices in US dollars for both countries?

Because we serve the United States and Mexico with the same team, the same tools and the same service, and we do not want a "cheaper in pesos" version. Mexican clients can pay in pesos at the exchange rate of the day.

What happens if there is an incident?

If you are a client with a plan, you call us and you get priority. Incident response is billed separately at $2,500 USD per day, minimum one day, and covers containment, analysis of what happened, recovery and a report. Vigía Plus clients already have a written response plan rehearsed in an annual drill.

Do you need write access to our systems?

No. Read-only only: a read-only user on GitHub or GitLab, a read-only SSH key, read access to your cloud and DNS. It is all written into the contract, you authorize every scan, and we never make changes to your systems.

Why a 3-month minimum?

The first month goes to onboarding and fixing what is urgent; monitoring shows its value once there is history to compare and the report shows a trend. Three months is the minimum for that; after that it is month to month.

Is there a discount?

Yes: 10% off with annual prepayment, on any plan.

Can we buy Onboarding on its own?

Yes. If you only want to know where you stand, Onboarding ($2,000 USD) is sold without a monthly plan and includes the inventory, the initial review and the prioritized report.

Is this the same as a pentest?

No. A pentest is a one-time test of one application by a specialist; monitoring runs every day and is read by a person every month. We quote pentests separately with a partner, per tester-day.
